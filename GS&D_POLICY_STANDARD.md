# GS&D Universal Public Policy Standard

Effective September 9, 2026.

All customer-facing GS&D sites, forms, tools, intake pages, payment pages, workflow products, diagnostic products, and digital services must expose or link to the same four policy surfaces: Website Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Refund & Cancellation Policy, and AI & Automated Systems Disclaimer.

Core protections: customer retains final decision responsibility unless a written scope expressly assigns implementation to GS&D; no guaranteed business/technical/financial/regulatory/repair/ranking/funding outcome; third-party outages, API changes, access restrictions, payment failures, vendor decisions, and platform policy changes remain third-party risks; GS&D scope is limited to expressly authorized work; GS&D retains pre-existing IP, systems, methods, workflows, templates, software, and proof-chain structures; liability is limited to the maximum extent permitted by law and aggregate liability for a specific engagement is capped at amounts actually paid for the specific services giving rise to the claim where legally enforceable; non-waivable rights remain intact; AI output requires verification and does not replace required authority or professional/manufacturer procedures; earned deposits/work and delivered digital materials are non-refundable to the extent permitted by law.

Canonical public policy files: terms.html, privacy.html, refunds.html, ai-disclaimer.html.
